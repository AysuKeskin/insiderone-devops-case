Rolling back the previous upgrade with a single command (make rollback).

--- 1. currently serving the new version ---
$ curl -s localhost:8090/version
{"built_at":"2026-08-21T20:07:20Z","commit":"bb60acc","version":"v0.1.1-rollout-demo"}


--- 2. roll back to the previous revision ---
$ helm rollback kube-pulse
Rollback was a success! Happy Helming!
$ kubectl rollout status deployment/kube-pulse
Waiting for deployment "kube-pulse" rollout to finish: 1 old replicas are pending termination...
Waiting for deployment "kube-pulse" rollout to finish: 1 old replicas are pending termination...
deployment "kube-pulse" successfully rolled out

--- 3. after: the previous version is back ---
$ curl -s localhost:8090/version
{"built_at":"2026-08-21T20:06:54Z","commit":"bb60acc","version":"v0.1.0-1-gbb60acc"}

$ helm history kube-pulse --max 3
