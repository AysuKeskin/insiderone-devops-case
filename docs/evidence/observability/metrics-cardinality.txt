$ curl -s localhost:8080/metrics | grep '^http_requests_total'
http_requests_total{method="GET",route="GET /healthz",status="200"} 138
http_requests_total{method="GET",route="GET /ping",status="200"} 39001
http_requests_total{method="GET",route="GET /readyz",status="200"} 274
http_requests_total{method="GET",route="GET /version",status="200"} 3
http_requests_total{method="GET",route="unmatched",status="404"} 105

Requests to 50 distinct unknown paths (/nope-1 .. /nope-50) all land on a
single route="unmatched" series. The metrics middleware labels by the
matched route pattern, never the raw path, so an attacker spraying random
URLs cannot grow the time series count.
